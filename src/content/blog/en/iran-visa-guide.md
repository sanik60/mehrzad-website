Most visitors need a visa to enter Iran. The usual route for tourists has three steps.

## 1. Get an authorisation code

A licensed Iranian travel agency applies for an **authorisation code** on your behalf. You send us a passport scan, a photo and your planned dates. Processing usually takes a few working days.

## 2. Apply for the visa

With the code you complete the e-visa application and collect the visa at the embassy or consulate you chose, or at the airport where this is available for your nationality.

## 3. Travel insurance

Valid travel insurance that covers Iran is required. We can arrange it for you.

> Visa rules change and depend on your nationality. Message us on WhatsApp before booking flights and we will confirm the current requirements for your passport.
