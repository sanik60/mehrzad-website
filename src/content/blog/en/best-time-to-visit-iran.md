Iran is a large country with very different climates, so the best time depends on what you want to do.

## Spring (March to May)

The most popular season. Mild weather in Isfahan, Shiraz and Yazd, and gardens in full bloom. Note that the Persian New Year (Nowruz, around 21 March) is a busy local holiday.

## Autumn (September to November)

Our favourite season for classic city tours: warm days, cool evenings and fewer crowds.

## Winter (December to February)

Ski season in the Alborz mountains near Tehran, with resorts such as Dizin and Shemshak. A good time for desert cities too.

## Summer (June to August)

Hot in the centre and south. Better for the cooler north-west, such as Tabriz and Kandovan.
